# Party Balloons & Event Services Website

Welcome to the **Party Balloons & Event Services** website! This multi-page website is designed to provide a seamless experience for browsing and booking party decorations and event services.

---

## Features

### Home Page

- A welcoming introduction with featured products, services, and highlights from recent events.

### Products & Services

- **Product Listings**: View a variety of balloons, party supplies, and decorations with clear pricing.
- **Service Packages**: Explore and book event planning services with transparent pricing and details.

### Gallery

- **Photo Wall**: A curated collection of images from past events, showcasing designs and setups.
- **Event Highlights**: View albums from specific events to inspire your next party.

### Contact Page

- **Contact Form**: Easily reach out for custom quotes, inquiries, or bookings.

### Responsive Design

- Fully optimized for viewing on desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.
